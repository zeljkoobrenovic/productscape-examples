Occupational Health and Safety Platform

Covers the modular, multilingual SaaS platform and implementation services that occupational health services, HSE and prevention teams, HR departments, managers and employees of large European organisations use to keep workers healthy, fit for work and safe: workforce and exposure data synchronised from HR, medical surveillance planning and appointment scheduling, clinical consultations with questionnaires, connected measurement devices and secure teleconsultation, a confidential occupational medical file, risk assessment and prevention plans, hazardous situations, inspections and action plans, chemical product tracking, work accident and occupational disease declaration with national reporting forms, employee and manager self-service portals, cross-module dashboards and regulatory reports, HDS and ISO 27001 certified hosting in the EEA, and a professional services team that configures, migrates, trains and supports every customer.
