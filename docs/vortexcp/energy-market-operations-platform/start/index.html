Energy Market Operations Platform

Covers the software and expertise-services platform that European energy suppliers, balance responsible parties, traders, portfolio operators and large consumers run their core market operations on: launching energy propositions and tariffs, onboarding and switching customers through market communication, collecting and validating meter data, billing and reconciliation, payments and debtor management, white-label customer self-service, sourcing, pricing and hedging, portfolio forecasting, TSO nominations and position management, intraday steering and imbalance settlement, customer trading, Dutch and Iberian market messaging, regulatory reporting, an open API, and market-entry, BRP-as-a-service and academy services across the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal and wider Europe.
