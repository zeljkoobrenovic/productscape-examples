# Payments and Revenue Infrastructure

Updated: 2026-11-15

This domain models Stripe as programmable payments and revenue infrastructure. Adyen is a detailed competitive comparison across online acceptance, unified commerce, platforms, money movement, billing and loyalty. Other alternatives are included where they change a customer’s operating model or responsibility.

## Customer value and responsibility

- Commerce teams convert demand into collected revenue while controlling loss, payment cost and store exceptions. Payments engineering, risk and store operators own distinct handoffs.
- Revenue teams release recurring and usage pricing with explainable invoices, credits, collections and accounting. Eligible merchant-of-record services are a separate choice from operating the sale and tax processes themselves.
- Platform teams activate legal counterparties, allocate funds and manage payout and liability exceptions. The seller/recipient is modeled separately because usable earnings and remediation are different jobs from platform monetization.
- Treasury and finance controllers own cash availability, funded obligations, entity/currency reconciliation and close approval. A payout acknowledgement is not a promise of usable recipient funds.

## Products and boundaries

The portfolio contains online payments, revenue automation, Connect platform payments, unified commerce, shared trust/finance capabilities, business Treasury/Global Payouts, and Managed Payments for eligible digital sellers. Every capability has a logical delivery and product association; optional functions retain their availability restrictions.

Business Treasury, Treasury for platforms, Connect payouts and standalone Global Payouts have different account, funding, jurisdiction and contractual requirements. Merchant-of-record, account-holder, funds-owner, recipient and loss-bearer roles must stay distinguishable. Preview, development and acquisition integration states are not general-availability promises.

Merchant inventory, fulfillment, loyalty systems, banks/rails and enterprise accounting are external contracts unless a specific product source establishes otherwise. This model does not attempt to describe Stripe or Adyen’s complete internal service topology, physical databases, cloud regions or universal legal rights. Agent-commerce channels and delegated treasury spending are separate flows; eligible automation preserves authority, approval, revocation and recovery.

## Strategic sequence

The modeled horizons start on 2026-11-15; they are authoring choices, not a published vendor roadmap.

1. By 2027-11-15, establish defensible measurement and complete activation, collection, payout, return and reconciliation paths before broad migration.
2. By 2029-11-15, expand eligible markets and financial/pricing models only after pilots demonstrate customer value and sustainable operating effort.
3. By 2031-11-15, automate supported commerce and financial actions with bounded authority, human exception handling and portable financial evidence.

Customer outcomes concern collected revenue, usable funds, correct bills and explained cash. Supplier outcomes concern retention, paid adoption and sustainable service operations. Their measures are related but not interchangeable. KPI definitions have owners, units and windows; no current values or numerical targets are asserted without a measurement source.

## Evidence and interpretation

Primary disclosures and integration contracts are stored in `customers/insights.json`, curated in `customers/links.json`, and attached to relevant bricks and streams. Financial statistics preserve their reported period, currency and business scope. FY2025 baselines and 2026 interim results are separate. Adyen’s Orb/Talon.One and Stripe’s Metronome acquisitions are distinguished from agreement-only watchlist items and from fully integrated features.

`REVIEW.md` preserves the original research assessment and records implementation verification separately. Logical records and ownership are inferred from the modeled jobs. Physical stores, retention durations and residency require product-specific evidence, so the catalog does not invent them. Residuality scenarios are hypotheses; a logical control does not establish that a vendor has survived an operational stress test.
