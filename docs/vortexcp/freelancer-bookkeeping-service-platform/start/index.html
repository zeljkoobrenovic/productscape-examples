Freelancer Bookkeeping Service Platform

Covers the subscription bookkeeping-as-a-service business that Dutch freelancers, sole proprietors, partnerships and small BVs use to keep a compliant administration without becoming bookkeepers themselves: a client web and mobile app for receipts (Scan & Herken), quotes and invoices, a PSD2 bank connection and a real-time dashboard; a bookkeeping engine that turns bookings into quarterly VAT returns, annual accounts, income tax and corporate tax returns filed with the Belastingdienst; a workspace in which internally trained personal bookkeepers review every administration, answer questions and keep deadlines; onboarding, switching and subscription billing at a fixed transparent yearly fee; and a partner programme with professional associations, insurers and pension providers for the creative and knowledge freelancer community.
