Paramedical and Mental Health Practice Platform

Covers the cloud practice software, patient environments and e-health that Dutch paramedical practices (physiotherapy, exercise therapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy, dietetics, rehabilitation) and mental health (GGZ) practices and institutions use to run care with less administration: agenda and online booking, the electronic patient record with discipline-specific care pathways and voice-to-text, measurement instruments and outcome monitoring (PROMs, ROM, LDF delivery), declarations to insurers through Vecozo under the GDS801 and zorgprestatiemodel rules, patient invoicing and payments, practice BI; a mental-health EPD with treatment plans, medication and youth-care contracting; the Uw Zorg Online and Quli patient environments (e-consult, appointments, repeat prescriptions, records, MedMij personal health environment) that connect 4 million people with GPs, pharmacies and therapists; the Embloom and Emogy e-health libraries for blended care; network integrations (ZorgDomein, ZorgMail, HIS/AIS connectors, partner API), practice websites and the HCI One consolidation of acquired labels on one NEN 7510 and ISO 27001 certified platform.
