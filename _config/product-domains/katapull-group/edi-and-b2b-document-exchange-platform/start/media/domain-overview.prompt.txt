Customers: Suppliers to Retail: Supplier Order Desk and Logistics Lead, Supplier Finance and Accounts Receivable Manager; IT and Integration: IT and ERP Manager at a Trading Company, ERP Implementation Partner; Retailers and Buying Organisations: Retail Supplier Onboarding and Supply Chain Manager
Products: Transus EDI Basic (Web EDI) (B2B SaaS - Web EDI portal): The EDI web portal for retail suppliers: exchange all commercial documents, automatic validation and real-time insight into messages; Transus EDI Plus (B2B SaaS - Integrated EDI): Everything in Basic plus EDI automation; Transus EDI Premium (B2B SaaS - Enterprise EDI): Everything in Plus with premium support, API connection with ERP and WMS systems, connection by certified Transus partners and invoice archiving; Transus E-Invoicing (B2B SaaS - E-invoicing compliance): E-invoicing within the EDI flow; Transus Vendor Managed Inventory (B2B SaaS - Collaboration): Supplier-managed replenishment from retailer stock and forecast data, used for example by Henri van de Bilt for Albert Heijn; Traci AI (AI agent - Partner onboarding): AI agent that recognises, interprets and links trading documents so new trading partners are connected without manual mapping; Transus Partner Network (Partner programme and API): Programme and API-first platform for ERP partners and Katapull Commerce Hub brands to resell and implement Transus EDI

Data: Network and Configuration Data, Business Document Data, Customer and Platform Data
Delivery channels: Transus EU Cloud: Document Exchange Core, Network and Onboarding Services, Transus Online Portal, AI Services, Security and Continuity; Integration Endpoints and Connectors: Customer Integration API and File Drop, Katapull Commerce Hub Connectors
