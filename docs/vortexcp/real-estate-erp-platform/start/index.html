Real Estate ERP Platform

Covers the ERP-style SaaS platform that real estate investors, asset managers, property management firms and owners' association (VvE) administrators run their portfolios on: objects and units, relations and CRM, letting and lease contracts with indexation and service charges, rent invoicing, bank reconciliation and multi-entity real estate accounting, technical management with fault tickets, work orders and multi-year maintenance plans, development projects, owners' association administration, dashboards and investor reporting, tenant, owner and supplier portals, hyper automation and AI assistants, and an open API with certified integrations across the Dutch and German real estate market.
