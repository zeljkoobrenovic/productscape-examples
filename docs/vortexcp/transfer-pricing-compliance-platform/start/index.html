Transfer Pricing Compliance Platform

Covers the SaaS platform and expert services that multinational tax teams, corporate legal counsel and tax advisory firms use to prepare, localise, file and defend three-tiered transfer pricing documentation: a rule-based transfer pricing model over legal entities and intercompany transactions, an automated Master File and Local File generator for 170+ countries, Country-by-Country Reporting including EU Public CbCR, benchmarks, a country regulations and deadline knowledge base, compliance workflow and dashboards, a legal module for entities, intercompany agreements and structure charts, AI assistance and machine translation, ERP and operational transfer pricing integrations, and a full-service documentation team that operates the same platform for outsourced clients.
