Draw the picture described below for the top of a product-domain website.
Domain: Occupational Health and Safety Platform
Reader: a product manager or engineer new to this domain who needs the big picture in ten seconds.

Background (for understanding only; do not write it in the image):
What the domain is: Covers the modular, multilingual SaaS platform and implementation services that occupational health services, HSE and prevention teams, HR departments, managers and employees of large European organisations use to keep workers healthy, fit for work and safe: workforce and exposure data synchronised from HR, medical surveillance planning and appointment scheduling, clinical consultations with questionnaires, connected measurement devices and secure teleconsultation, a confidential occupational medical file, risk assessment and prevention plans, hazardous situations, inspections and action plans, chemical product tracking, work accident and occupational disease declaration with national reporting forms, employee and manager self-service portals, cross-module dashboards and regulatory reports, HDS and ISO 27001 certified hosting in the EEA, and a professional services team that configures, migrates, trains and supports every customer.
Customers: Occupational Health Services: Occupational Physician, Occupational Health Nurse / Medical Assistant, Occupational Health Service Manager / Coordinator; HSE and Prevention: HSE Manager / Prevention Adviser, Site / Line Manager; HR and Employees: HR Administrator / Personnel Officer, Employee; Kitry Services and Content Teams: Kitry Implementation and Customer Success Consultant
Products: Kitry Occupational Health (B2B Vertical Health SaaS Module): The core module of the Kitry platform and the system of record of an employer's internal or autonomous occupational health service; Kitry Workplace Safety (B2B Vertical Safety SaaS Module): The prevention module for HSE managers and prevention advisers; Kitry Work Accidents and Occupational Diseases (B2B Vertical Compliance SaaS Module): The event module that takes a work accident, commuting accident or suspected occupational disease from first report to declaration, analysis and follow-up; Kitry Employee and Manager Portals (B2B2E Self-service Portals): Role-based self-service portals that extend the platform to every employee and manager of the organisation; Kitry Monitoring, KPIs and Regulatory Reports (B2B Analytics SaaS Module (transversal)): The transversal monitoring module that reads across occupational health, workplace safety and accident data; Kitry Teleconsultation (B2B Integrated Telehealth Service): Secure teleconsultation integrated into the consultation workspace and the employee portal and delivered through the Vonage video platform; Kitry Ecosystem Integrations (B2B Integration and Connectivity Layer): The connectivity layer that keeps the platform in sync with the customer's systems and the partner ecosystem; Kitry SaaS Hosting (Shared and Dedicated HDS) (B2B Certified SaaS Hosting Tier): The hosting tier of every Kitry subscription; Kitry Implementation and Services (Professional Services): The services model that takes every customer live and keeps it current
Product bricks: 36 building blocks in groups: Occupational Health, Platform Foundation, Workforce and Exposure Data, Workplace Safety
Data: Workforce and Exposure Data, Occupational Health, Workplace Safety, Accidents and Occupational Diseases, Portals and Insight, Platform and Services
Delivery channels: Web Application: Workforce and Surveillance Planning, Occupational Health Consultation, Occupational Medical Records, Workplace Safety, Work Accidents and Occupational Diseases, Monitoring and Analytics, Administration and Configuration; Portals and Mobile: Employee Portal, Manager Portal, Responsive Field Safety Reporting; Integrations: HRIS Synchronisation, SSO Identity Providers, Goodflag Electronic Signature, Vonage Teleconsultation, Cegedim SMS, Measurement Devices, National Reporting Connectors; Hosting Environments: Shared HDS SaaS, Dedicated HDS SaaS; Internal Operations: Implementation and Migration Workspace, Regulatory Content Back Office, Support Desk and Account Management
External systems: Sales channels: HRIS event webhooks, Screening stations on the consultation site, National bodies and insurers, Partner webhooks; Service: Ameli / net-entreprises.fr, Fedris, Belgian work accident insurers, Luxembourg AAA and Dutch labour inspectorate; Data: Customer SFTP and file exchange, National health record and interoperability services, SDS provider services and supplier portals, Official legal sources; Integration: HRIS platforms, Measurement device vendors
Competitors: 12 players, for example Cority, Enablon, Intelex

Composition:
One wide "context map" read from left to right:
- Left, outside the boundary: each customer group as ONE small cluster of two or three generic people
  with exactly one label, the group name. Do not split a group into individual roles or label single people.
- Center: one large rounded boundary for the domain. Inside it, the products as a row of distinct
  product cards on top; below them ONE shared row of building-block piles, one pile per product brick
  group, spanning the full width under all products. Products are assembled from many groups and groups
  are shared by many products, so do not pair a product with a group: no columns and no product-to-group
  arrows; the product row simply rests on the brick row. Below the bricks a shared data layer (a simple
  database shape) used by all brick groups.
- Along the bottom edge of the boundary: the delivery channels as a base strip of runtime platforms
  (servers, cloud, app stores) that the bricks are deployed to and the products are delivered through.
- Right, outside the boundary: the external systems as separate service tiles, each connected to the
  boundary with a two-way connector line.
- Bottom right, set apart and smaller: competitors as alternative storefronts the customers could choose instead,
  with a thin dashed line towards the customers, not connected to the domain.
- Arrows: customers <-> products; the brick row reads and writes the data layer; bricks are deployed onto the delivery channels; the domain exchanges data with the external systems. No other arrows.

Style:
- Aspect ratio 16:9, generous margins, everything inside the frame.
- Background: pure white (#FFFFFF) across the whole canvas, with no cream, beige, paper tint,
  texture, or vignette. Only the domain boundary and the tiles may have a light fill.
- Clean flat editorial illustration with isometric hints: crisp navy outlines, restrained teal, blue,
  and warm amber accents, soft shadows, no photorealism and no 3D rendering.
- Outside elements and inside elements must be clearly distinguishable: the boundary separates them.
- Labels in a clear sans-serif, large enough to read at half size on a laptop screen.
- Generic, inclusive people and generic devices. No brand logos, trademarks, or watermarks anywhere,
  even for the named competitors, sales channels, and external systems: draw generic icons
  (a shopping cart, a storefront, a truck) instead of company marks. This includes app-store, browser,
  payment-card, and social-network marks, and product cards drawn as wordmarks.
