Household Finance Brokerage Platform

Covers the omnichannel comparison and brokerage business that Belgian households use to take control of their recurring costs: online simulators and comparison modules for mortgage loans, insurance (car, fire, funeral, outstanding-balance), energy and telecom; a network of about 70 franchised hypotheek.winkel offices where mortgage experts compare the conditions of 25 banks and guide buyers from feasibility certificate to notary deed; customer-care centres that compare, switch and cancel insurance, energy and telecom contracts by phone and chat; contract monitoring that warns households when a better deal exists; an academy that trains scarce mortgage and insurance advisors; and partner, tariff and commission operations with more than 50 banks, insurers and providers who pay the referral fees that keep the service free for consumers.
