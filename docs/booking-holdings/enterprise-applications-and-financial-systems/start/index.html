Enterprise Applications and Financial Systems

The Enterprise Applications and Financial Systems domain covers Booking Holdings' global finance ecosystem as seen by its architecture function: the SAP S/4HANA finance core and high-volume subledger, payments-to-ledger for card and alternative payment methods, partner settlement, source-to-pay, tax and treasury, close and SOX controls, finance data and integration, and the architecture governance that converges brand finance technology onto one compliant, scalable platform.
