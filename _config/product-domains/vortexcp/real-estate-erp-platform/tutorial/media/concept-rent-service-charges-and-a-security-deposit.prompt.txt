Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: one illustration in a plain-language product-domain tutorial for adults.
Domain: Keeping property, people, money, and maintenance connected
Reader: A curious adult from another field. No knowledge of property management, accounting, tenancy law, or business software is assumed.
Scope: An introduction to the ongoing management of property and the software supporting it. The recurring example is a Dutch apartment building with rental homes and an owners' association. Commercial leases and other countries, including Germany, have different rules. Buying an investment, mortgage advice, and detailed legal procedures are outside this guide. Canal Court, its people, contracts, prices, and financial examples are fictional.
Subject: Rent, service charges, and a security deposit
Meaning: Rent pays for use of the premises. Service charges cover specified services or costs where the agreement and rules allow them, often through advances followed by a settlement. A security deposit is held as security and dealt with under the applicable conditions; it is not ordinary rent income.
Concrete example: Sam's illustrative monthly charge is €900 rent plus a €100 service-cost advance. A security deposit is recorded separately. If a year's advances total €1,200 and the permitted costs properly allocated to Sam are €1,080, the €120 difference is a credit to settle.
Why this matters: Money can have different purposes even when the same organization administers it. An owner's full association contribution cannot simply be copied into the tenant's service charges.

Teaching scene:
Three separate equally sized labeled containers, with no connecting arrows and no shared profit total. Container 1 'Rent' shows apartment keys. Container 2 'Service advances' shows a communal light and cleaning equipment beside a review document. Container 3 'Security deposit' shows a secure folder with a return-arrow icon. Bottom label 'Different purposes, separate records'. NO currency amounts, percentages, refunds promised unconditionally, money-growing plants, or words such as profit or revenue. Exactly four labels. Clear adult diagram on warm white.

Style and composition:
- Aspect ratio 16:9, generous margins, bright warm-white background.

  restrained teal, blue, and warm amber accents, subtle texture, very little shading.

- Use an inclusive cast without occupational or cultural stereotypes.

  explicitly requested in the teaching scene; otherwise keep the image free of text.

- Explain the idea visually. The surrounding tutorial provides its full definition and caveats.
