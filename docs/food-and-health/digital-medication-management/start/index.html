Digital Medication Management

The Digital Medication Management domain covers the patient, carer, care-provider, and pharmacy workflows required to run an automated medicine-dispensing and remote medication-management service for home care and supported living — spanning the in-home dispensing device, pharmacy multi-dose pouch logistics, remote adherence monitoring, missed-dose alarm escalation, care-team portals, and the regulated medical-device software platform behind them.
