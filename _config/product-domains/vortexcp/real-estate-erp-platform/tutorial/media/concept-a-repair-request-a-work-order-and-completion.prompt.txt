Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: one illustration in a plain-language product-domain tutorial for adults.
Domain: Keeping property, people, money, and maintenance connected
Reader: A curious adult from another field. No knowledge of property management, accounting, tenancy law, or business software is assumed.
Scope: An introduction to the ongoing management of property and the software supporting it. The recurring example is a Dutch apartment building with rental homes and an owners' association. Commercial leases and other countries, including Germany, have different rules. Buying an investment, mortgage advice, and detailed legal procedures are outside this guide. Canal Court, its people, contracts, prices, and financial examples are fictional.
Subject: A repair request, a work order, and completion
Meaning: A request describes a problem. A work order instructs an agreed task, with a location, responsible party, and scope. Completion means checking the agreed result and recording any remaining work. The request, repair, supplier bill, and payment are linked but separate records.
Concrete example: Sam reports water on the ceiling. An inspection identifies a roof problem. Sending a contractor does not prove that the leak and any agreed follow-up work have been resolved.
Why this matters: Closing a ticket too early makes the dashboard look better while the person in the home still has a problem.

Teaching scene:
A very simple four-scene teaching diagram. The ONLY words anywhere in the image are four large headings: 'Report', 'Authorize', 'Repair', 'Verify'. Beneath Report: a tenant points to a ceiling drip. Beneath Authorize: one person checks one sheet bearing ONLY a large roof icon and three abstract horizontal lines. Beneath Repair: a worker holds a tool beside a small roof model on a table. Beneath Verify: the same tenant and an inspector look at a dry ceiling. Three simple arrows connect the scenes from left to right. Leave generous white space. Absolutely no extra documents, text on documents, tiny letters, signatures, subheadings, captions, serial numbers, invoices, money or text decorations. Use clean flat navy teal amber adult illustration. These four scenes alone teach the distinction; surrounding prose supplies details.
